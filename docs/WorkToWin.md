## 🌟 Overview

The Work-to-Win ecosystem creates a sustainable economic model where every participant contributes value and receives appropriate rewards through the $NEURONS token system. This document outlines the value flows and incentive structures for all stakeholders.

## 👥 Stakeholder Roles

### 👨‍💼 Admin / Financial

**Value Source:**
- Share of course/subscription fees
- Revenue from discounts paid in $NEURONS (partial burn, other part to treasury)
- Treasury control (allocation in liquidity pools, yield, NRS treasury)

**Returns:**
- Platform maintenance covered by fees
- Positive margin if course + subscription volume is high
- Indirect ROI: $NEURONS token appreciation over time

---

### 🎓 Students

**Value Source:**
- Earn $NEURONS by studying (PoK mining)
- Receive discounts by paying in NRS
- NFT certificates may have symbolic/collectible value

**Returns:**
- Lower learning cost
- Token that can be used to pay for new courses or traded (when liquid)
- Gain reputation (certificates + XP) → can become tutor

---

### 📚 Tutors / Teachers

**Value Source:**
- Direct revenue from enrollments/subscriptions (revenue share)
- Part in stablecoins (USDC/T), part in NRS
- Premium badges/NFT for students (upsell model)

**Returns:**
- Recurring revenue flow (subscriptions)
- Potential appreciation of received NRS
- Autonomy: publish courses on demand and price them

---

### 🛡️ Moderators / Curators

**Value Source:**
- NRS rewards for each validated course / removed fraud
- Bounties paid by DAO or Treasury

**Returns:**
- Continuous activity = token income
- Recognition → possible progression to Admin

---

### 🔍 PoK Auditors

**Value Source:**
- NRS remuneration for auditing epochs and confirming claims
- Additional reward if they detect fraud

**Returns:**
- Constant flow (every epoch needs auditing)
- Gain influence in DAO (credibility + reputation)

---

### 💸 Investors / Sponsors

**Value Source:**
- Can finance scholarships: buy courses in bulk and donate to students → receive extra NRS (incentive)
- Participate in DAO revenue share: % of revenue in NRS or USDC
- Increased demand for NRS → token appreciation
- NFT Sponsor Badge (premium access, community visibility)

**Returns:**
- **Double ROI:**
  - Financial (revenue, token appreciation)
  - Marketing (brand tied to education, ESG/social impact)

---

### 🗳️ DAO / Governance

**Value Source:**
- Decides emission parameters, discounts and budgets
- Controls treasury funds

**Returns:**
- Successful delegates influence more rewards
- Active participation generates treasury bounties
- Token holders win if ecosystem appreciates

---

### ⚙️ Relayer / Infra Operators

**Value Source:**
- Receive on-chain fees for executing cross-chain events
- Direct payments from Treasury to operate critical infrastructure

**Returns:**
- Predictable revenue in NRS/USDC (maintenance contract)
