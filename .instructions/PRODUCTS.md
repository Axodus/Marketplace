# Marketplace Products

# Purpose

Marketplace products coordinate:
- NFT assets and collections
- native products
- native assets
- external assets
- external collections
- federated products and assets
- ecosystem access
- digital services
- operational infrastructure
- ACS systems
- educational systems
- licenses
- subscriptions
- enterprise offers
- tenant catalogs
- white-label marketplace offers
- partner and affiliate offers

Products represent operational ecosystem capabilities.

---

# Product Categories

## Native Products
- products originated inside Axodus infrastructure
- products licensed or operated by Axodus systems
- products controlled through Axodus governance and operational boundaries
- products with explicit Axodus ownership, entitlement, subscription or licensing context

Native Products may still require governance, billing, treasury, entitlement and telemetry checks before any future execution.

---

## Native Assets
- assets originated inside Axodus infrastructure
- assets issued, controlled, licensed or operated by Axodus systems
- assets whose source, lifecycle and operational authority are native to Axodus

Native Assets must not be confused with external or federated assets.

---

## NFT Products
- ERC721 assets
- ERC1155 assets
- collections
- seller profiles
- asset registry records
- EIP-2981 royalty metadata
- fixed listings
- bids
- english auctions
- dutch auctions
- buy-now previews

NFT products are the first commercial vertical of Marketplace and remain the foundation for asset ownership, collection distribution, royalty compatibility and marketplace discovery.

---

## External Assets
- NFTs originated from external contracts
- certificates originated outside Axodus infrastructure
- licenses originated outside Axodus infrastructure
- digital assets discovered through external wallets, external collections or Federation Providers

External Assets may be represented in Marketplace for visibility, interoperability, discovery, import and progressive validation. Displaying an External Asset does not imply native issuance, custody, liquidation, full validation, commercial guarantee, royalty execution, settlement or Axodus infrastructure responsibility.

External Assets must expose:
- origin
- provider
- validation status
- provenance
- risk classification
- trust boundaries

---

## External Collections
- collections originated outside native Axodus infrastructure
- collections discovered through future provider, wallet, contract or catalog workflows
- collections that may become visible, searchable, referencable, classified or displayed in Marketplace

External Collections must not be displayed as native Axodus collections.

---

## Federated Products And Assets
- products or assets represented through Marketplace Federation Domain
- external or mixed-origin items with explicit origin and provider context
- items that may be visible before full validation is complete
- items that require validation status and trust boundaries before user-facing classification

Federated Products and Federated Assets can be displayed in Marketplace without implying native Axodus issuance, custody, liquidation, commercial guarantee, complete validation, royalty execution, bridge execution or settlement.

Federated Products and Federated Assets must carry:
- origin
- provider
- validation status
- provenance
- risk and authenticity signals
- trust boundaries

# Product Architecture Domains

Products must map cleanly into the Phase 00 domains:
- Marketplace Federation Domain
- Marketplace-as-a-Service Domain
- Distribution Network Domain
- Revenue Sharing Domain
- Marketplace Intelligence Domain

The NFT product vertical must remain compatible with all five domains without losing its ERC721, ERC1155, EIP-2981, listing, bid, auction, buy-now, seller, collection and asset registry foundations.

Marketplace Federation Domain product concepts include:
- External Collections
- External Assets
- External Contracts
- External Metadata
- Federation Providers
- Federated Assets
- External Asset Validation

---

# Constraints

- no opaque products
- no hidden operational dependencies
- no governance bypass
- no hidden ranking authority
- no real billing or payout execution in Phase 00
- no wallet signatures or on-chain execution in Phase 00
- no external partner or affiliate integration in Phase 00
- no external asset is trusted by default
- no external asset may be displayed as native Axodus
- no federated product implies custody, liquidation, settlement, bridge execution or commercial guarantee by default
