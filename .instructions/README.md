# Axodus Marketplace Workspace

# Purpose

The Marketplace workspace is the ecosystem distribution and commercial access infrastructure of Axodus.

Marketplace coordinates:
- digital products
- ACS services
- educational products
- DAO services
- subscriptions
- licensing
- enterprise offerings
- token utility
- ecosystem commerce

Marketplace is not a traditional ecommerce platform.

Marketplace is sovereign ecosystem distribution infrastructure.

---

# Marketplace Philosophy

Marketplace exists to distribute ecosystem capabilities.

The objective is to connect:
- users
- DAOs
- enterprises
- ACS systems
- Academy systems
- operational infrastructure

through:
- transparent access
- sustainable commerce
- governance-aware distribution
- treasury-compatible settlements

---

# Governance Relationship

Marketplace remains governance-aware.

Governance defines:
- marketplace policies
- treasury boundaries
- operational restrictions
- licensing constraints
- constitutional limits

Marketplace systems must never bypass governance authority.

---

# ACS Relationship

Marketplace integrates deeply with ACS.

Marketplace may distribute:
- MCP deployments
- AI agents
- orchestration packages
- workflow systems
- cognitive infrastructure
- AI-powered services

ACS services remain bounded and observable.

---

# Academy Relationship

Marketplace integrates with Academy through:
- educational access
- certifications
- learning paths
- governance formation products
- premium educational systems

Knowledge becomes distributed ecosystem infrastructure.

---

# Business Relationship

Marketplace integrates with Business through:
- enterprise provisioning
- operational subscriptions

# Defi Relationship

Marketplace integrates with Defi through:
