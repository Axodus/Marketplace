Marketplace Flow

How it works

  1. PartnerA company, DAO or developer joins the ecosystem.
  2. Publish ProductA digital offering or service is published.
  3. Marketplace ValidationCompliance, governance and quality validation occurs.
  4. DistributionThe offering reaches sales channels and partners.
  5. Customer AccessCustomers discover and acquire the offering.
  6. Subscription / LicenseSubscription and license delivery is managed.
  7. SupportContinuous support and operational lifecycle.

Frequently asked questions

What is Axodus Marketplace?

A distribution infrastructure for digital products, AI services, subscriptions, licenses and enterprise solutions across the Axodus ecosystem.

Can I sell products?

Yes. The Marketplace enables companies, projects and developers to publish and distribute digital offerings with governance and control.

Can companies join?

Companies can join as partners or request custom deployments inside or outside the Axodus ecosystem.

How do subscriptions work?

Subscriptions are managed as recurring offerings with renewal, licensing and consumption metrics.
